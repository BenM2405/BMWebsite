DS3 DataHacks 2025

DataHacks 2025 was a hackathon hosted by UCSD's Data Science Student Society (DS3). I worked on a team with my friends Teo and Arnav, where we fed Pokémon soundtracks and their real-world counterparts into the Audio Spectrogram Transformer (AST) to analyze how much the composers drew from the regions that inspired the games. I compiled all the soundtracks and music, and built the frontend using Streamlit to present our project. You can check out the project at the link below!

Incessant

We created Incessant during a 48-hour game jam, with the theme "On Repeat". We leaned into the idea of repetition by creating a game that reflects the monotony of office life, with each in-game day presenting the same routine, with a new task layered in daily. I worked on both the programming and art alongside Teo who contributed to the music and art as well as Arnav who contributed to the programming. You can check out our game below!
